07 · FAQ

Quietly answered.

How do I place an order?

Add pieces to your bag, then tap “Order on WhatsApp.” Your cart is auto-formatted into a message — we confirm and dispatch.

Is the promo price the final price?

Yes. The crossed-out standard price is the reference value; the highlighted promo price is what you pay.

Do watches come with a box and adjuster?

Every timepiece ships with original packaging, presentation bag, and a complimentary sizing adjuster.

Can I customize or request a piece not listed?

Absolutely. Reach out via WhatsApp — our concierge sources signature pieces on request.

What is the return window?

Seven days from delivery for unworn pieces in original packaging. We coordinate pickup.
